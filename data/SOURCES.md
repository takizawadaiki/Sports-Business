# Data sources

Retrieved 16 November 2026. Every figure on the page comes from one of these.

| File | Figures | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `caps.csv` | NBA salary cap by season | NBA press releases (pr.nba.com, 30 June 2025 for 2025-26); earlier seasons and 2026-27 as listed by SalarySwish with the date each was confirmed |
| `caps.csv` | NFL salary cap by year | NFL announcements as reported by National Football Post (2025 and 2026) and the table in Wikipedia's "Salary cap" article (2015 to 2024) |
| `caps.csv` | NHL upper and lower limits | NHL and NHLPA announcement of 31 January 2025 (2025-26 to 2027-28) and PuckPedia's salary cap history |
| `leagues.csv` | NBA tax line, aprons, minimum team salary, maximum salary | NBA press release of 30 June 2025; Hoops Rumors, same date |
| `leagues.csv` | Players' share of revenue | NBA: 49 to 51% of basketball-related income under the agreements since 2011. NFL: 48%, and at least 48.5% in a 17-game season (Front Office Sports, 15 March 2020). NHL: 50% of hockey-related revenue (US Bureau of Labor Statistics, Monthly Labor Review, 2013) |
| `leagues.csv` | League revenue | NFL: $21.2 billion in the 2024 season (Forbes, 29 August 2025). NBA: about $12.5 billion in 2024-25 (Forbes, 23 October 2025). MLB: $12.5 billion, the sum of Forbes's 30 team estimates for 2025 (20 March 2026). NHL: about $7.9 billion, the sum of Forbes's 32 team estimates for 2024-25 (11 December 2025) as listed on Wikipedia |
| `leagues.csv` | MLB luxury tax threshold | $241 million in 2025, from the 2022 to 2026 Basic Agreement |
| `leagues.csv` | Roster sizes | Each league's collective agreement: 15 standard contracts (NBA), 53 (NFL), 23 (NHL), 26 (MLB) |
| `top_contracts.csv` | Highest-paid players | NBA: Hoops Rumors, top 50 salaries for 2025-26. NFL: Sports Illustrated, highest-paid players by average annual value, 2025. NHL: TSN, highest cap hits for 2025-26. MLB: the league's luxury-tax value for Shohei Ohtani's contract |
| `balance.csv` | Spread of winning percentages and year-to-year repeat | Calculated in my salary cap project from 26 seasons of standings (takizawadaiki.github.io/salary-cap/) |

League revenue figures are estimates by Forbes, not audited accounts, and they are broader than the revenue each league's agreement shares with players.

Removed from the earlier version of this page because no reliable source could be found: team-by-team payrolls for the NBA, NFL and NHL, playoff-efficiency scores, Gini and Lorenz figures built on partial salary lists, revenue-per-player figures, and all forecasts.
