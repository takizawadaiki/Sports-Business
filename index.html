The NBA, NFL, NHL and MLB each split money between owners and players in a different way. This page sets the four systems side by side: the rules, how fast the caps have grown, how much one star can take, and whether any of it makes the leagues more even.

06MethodSources and limits

Where the numbers come from

  • Caps, floors and tax lines. League announcements: the NBA’s release of 30 June 2025, the NHL and NHLPA’s payroll ranges of 31 January 2025, and the NFL’s yearly cap notices. Earlier seasons are from SalarySwish, PuckPedia and Wikipedia’s salary cap tables.
  • Players’ share. Each league’s collective agreement, as reported by Front Office Sports (NFL) and the US Bureau of Labor Statistics (NHL, with the NBA for comparison).
  • Top contracts. Hoops Rumors (NBA salaries for 2025–26), Sports Illustrated (NFL, average per year), TSN (NHL cap hits). Ohtani’s figure is the league’s own luxury-tax value of his deferred contract.
  • Revenue. Forbes: league totals for the NFL and NBA, and the sum of its team estimates for MLB and the NHL.
  • Balance. Calculated in my salary cap project from 26 seasons of standings in each league.

What to keep in mind

  • Revenue is estimated. Leagues do not publish accounts. Forbes’s totals are also broader than the revenue each agreement shares with players, so the cap is not simply the players’ share of these figures.
  • A cap is not a payroll. NBA teams can exceed the cap and many do. NFL cap hits can be moved between years. Only the NHL’s cap is close to what teams actually spend.
  • Top contracts are measured differently. The NBA and NHL figures are this season’s salary or cap hit. The NFL figure is a contract’s yearly average, because one season’s cap hit can be rearranged.
  • Balance has other causes. Basketball has five players on the floor and one star decides more. A cap is one reason leagues differ, not the only one.
  • What was removed. An earlier version of this page had team payrolls, efficiency scores and inequality measures that could not be traced to reliable sources. They are gone.
